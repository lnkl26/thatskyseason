Bean's Wardrobe!

Here you will find all the outfit and cosmetics that Bean owns and switches to from time to time.

Designed and drawn by @kainoshu ⟡

Bean main design and color palette.
Bean is a curious, energetic chibi moth, with two side ponytails and a double fluffy "ahoge" on their head. The cape is inspired by the Sparrow cape from the game, but the colors remind those of a blue jay instead. The outfit is a call-back to the original moth outfit, but the shorts and the boots are a dark blue-ish color.
